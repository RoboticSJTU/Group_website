Our three accepted papers are:

**Paper 1: PO-PDDL — Learning Symbolic POMDPs from Visual Demonstrations for Robot Planning Under Uncertainty**
- **Paper**: [Available on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.15654)
- **Project Page**: [PO-PDDL Project Website](https://roboticsjtu.github.io/PO-PDDL/)

**Paper 2: I-Perceive — A Foundation Model for Vision-Language Active Perception**
- **Paper**: [Available on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.00600)
- **Project Page**: [I-Perceive Project Website](https://roboticsjtu.github.io/I-Perceive-Page/)

**Paper 3: TGPO — Trace-Guided Policy Optimization for Robot Task Planning via Verifiable Subgoal Generation**
- **Paper**: [Available on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12244v2)
- **Project Page**: [TGPO Project Website](https://tgpo2026.github.io/TGPO/)

We look forward to presenting our work and sharing our findings with the broader research community at NeurIPS 2026. Stay tuned for more details about the papers and presentations as we approach the conference!
